Austin Water — Grease Trap Maintenance

Austin, TX grease trap / FOG rules

Not legal advice. Last verified 2026-09-22 · confidence high. Confirm with the city before you set a contract.

Pumping / cleaning

Minimum interval: 90 days.

Accumulation rule: Clean more often if grease and solids are 50% or more of wetted height (bottom of trap to invert of outlet).

Records: keep required records about 3 years on site.

Hauler: City of Austin-permitted liquid waste hauler only.

Permit: Follow Austin Water FOG / trap registration rules for your site.

Enforcement: Fines and permit action are possible. Confirm current penalties with Austin Water / 311.

Hood cleaning

NFPA 96 frequencies (widely republished by fire departments): solid fuel monthly; high-volume quarterly; moderate semiannual; low-volume annual. Insurers and local fire inspectors can be stricter.

Pumping / cleaning

Minimum interval: No fixed calendar interval in the sources we cited.

Accumulation rule: Chicago plumbing code requires interceptors to be maintained 'as often as necessary' — not a fixed 90-day clock. Keep written maintenance records for the life of the unit or at least 5 years, whichever is longer.

Records: keep manifests about 5 years on site.

Hauler: Use a licensed waste hauler and keep manifests.

Permit: Food establishments are inspected by CDPH. Interceptor rules sit in the plumbing / water code.

Enforcement: Food inspection failures (pests, grease, sanitation) are public. Plumbing/FOG citations are separate. Confirm current penalties.

Hood cleaning

NFPA 96 frequencies (widely republished by fire departments): solid fuel monthly; high-volume quarterly; moderate semiannual; low-volume annual. Insurers and local fire inspectors can be stricter.

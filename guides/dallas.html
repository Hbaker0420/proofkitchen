Dallas Water Utilities — Liquid Waste / Pretreatment

Dallas, TX grease trap / FOG rules

Not legal advice. Last verified 2026-09-21 · confidence high. Confirm with the city before you set a contract.

Pumping / cleaning

Minimum interval: 90 days.

Accumulation rule: Pump sooner whenever floating FOG plus settled solids reach 25% of design capacity / liquid depth.

Records: keep required records about 3 years on site.

Hauler: City of Dallas-permitted liquid waste transporter. Keep manifests on site.

Permit: Liquid waste generator requirements per Chapter 19 — confirm your permit status.

Enforcement: Inspectors issue citations for missed pumping, bad manifests, or over-25% accumulation. Confirm current fine schedule.

Hood cleaning

NFPA 96 frequencies (widely republished by fire departments): solid fuel monthly; high-volume quarterly; moderate semiannual; low-volume annual. Insurers and local fire inspectors can be stricter.

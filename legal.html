Terms & privacy

ProofKitchen provides general information and a record-keeping tool. It is not legal, compliance, or professional advice. City codes change. Verify with the authority having jurisdiction.

The free logbook stores data in your browser (localStorage). We do not receive it unless you later use a cloud Pro account.

Inspection search queries official NYC and Chicago open-data APIs from your browser. Their terms apply.

Waitlist emails collected after launch will be used only to notify you about ProofKitchen billing and product updates.

No fabricated reviews, no fake customers, no impersonation of inspectors.

ProofKitchen is an information tool, not a lawyer, inspector, or city agency. Always keep original manifests and confirm rules with the AHJ. Data: NYC DOHMH and Chicago CDPH open data; city ordinance pages cited on each guide.
